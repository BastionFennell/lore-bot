## Description

Veena is one of the capitals of the Twins' Isle, named after its ruler, Queen Veena. Veena and King {{varik}} are twins who once ruled the isle together peacefully. After a fight between the twins escalated, it became an all-out jungle war between the two cities, which is currently ongoing.

## Tidebinding

Veena is one of the checkpoints on the {{tidal-schools}} transfer route. This gives it a higher concentration of {{kin}} and {{tidebound}} than {{varik}}, along with a greater dependence on {{tidebinding}} as a way of life.
