## Description

Varik is one of the capitals of the Twins' Isle, named after its ruler, King Varik. The king is gluttonous but generous. Varik is currently engaged in an ongoing jungle war with the neighboring city of {{veena}}, ruled by King Varik's twin, Queen Veena.

## Economy

Varik's main export is fruit harvested from the island's jungles, prized for its wide range of medicinal properties. The city has recently discovered an ore deposit, which it has been using to fuel its war effort against {{veena}}.

The economy is centralized: everyone contributes to government-owned community centers, from which they can draw whatever resources they need, such as food and clothing. Outside of this system, people often barter goods and services rather than using gold.
